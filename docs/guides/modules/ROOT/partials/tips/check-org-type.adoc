To find out your organization type, check your organization slug at menu:Org[Overview]. `github` and `bitbucket` type organizations are OAuth authenticated orgs and the organization slug is structured as follows:

* A `github` type organization has a slug of the form `github/<your-org-name>` or `gh/<your-org-name>`
* A `bitbucket` type organization has a slug of the form `bitbucket/<your-org-name>` or `bb/<your-org-name>`
* A `circleci` type organization has a slug of the form `circleci/<UID>`
