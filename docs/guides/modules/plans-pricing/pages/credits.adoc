[#overview]
== Overview

Credits are used on CircleCI's Free, Performance, and Scale Plans. Each plan offers key CI/CD features, and some plans offer customization options depending on your needs.

The right plan for you and your team will depend on several factors:

- How many users are on your team
- How much support you need (community and free support, standard support, or premium support)
- If you want access to different machine types and resource classes
- If you want a limited or unlimited number of self-hosted runners

View the xref:plan-overview.adoc[Plans Overview] page for details on what features are available per plan, including the Free, Performance, Scale, and Server plans.

Take a look at the CircleCI link:https://circleci.com/pricing/[Pricing] page, and the link:https://circleci.com/product/features/resource-classes/[resource class comparison table], to learn more about how credits are distributed across different machine types and resource classes.

[#managing-credit-usage]
== Managing credit usage

Properly managing network and storage usage can potentially lower the amount of credits used per month. If you would like to find out more about managing network and storage usage, see the xref:optimize:persist-data.adoc[Persisting Data] page.

For detailed cost tracking and analysis, you can integrate with Vantage or Finout to monitor CI/CD spending alongside your cloud infrastructure and SaaS tools. See the xref:integrate-with-vantage-for-cost-management.adoc[Integrate CircleCI With Vantage] and xref:integrate-with-finout-for-cost-management.adoc[Integrate CircleCI With Finout] guides for more information.

[#open-source-credit-usage]
== Open source credit usage

Organizations on our Free Plan can use up to 400,000 credits per month, for free, for Linux open source builds. Using our Free Plan and keeping your repository public will enable this for you. Open source credit availability and limits are not visible in the CircleCI web app.

If you build on macOS, we also offer organizations on our Free Plan 30,000 credits every month to use on macOS open source builds. To find out more, visit the CircleCI link:https://support.circleci.com/hc/en-us[support portal], which includes an Ask AI option to get you the information you need.

[#free-plan-limits]
== Free plan limits

Organizations on the Free Plan receive 30,000 credits per month for personal (non-open source) usage. Free organizations also receive an included allotment of 1 GB of network transfer and 2 GB-months of storage. Network and storage usage beyond these allotments draws from your monthly credits rather than being billed separately.

When your available credits reach 0, personal builds are blocked until your credits refill at the start of the next month. Open source builds are not subject to this block and continue to run even if your credit balance is exhausted.

[#credit-expiry]
== Credit expiry

On the Performance Plan, credits roll over each month and expire one year after purchase. Unused credits are forfeited when the account subscription is canceled.

[#when-credits-run-out]
== When your credits run out

What happens when your credit balance reaches 0 depends on your plan.

[#performance-plan-refills]
=== Performance plan

When you reach 0 credits, CircleCI refills approximately 25% of your credit subscription, with a minimum refill of 25,000 credits. For example, if your monthly package size is 100,000 credits, you are automatically refilled 25,000 credits when you reach 0 remaining credits.

If your account receives repeated refills, review your credit usage in the CircleCI web app by selecting menu:Plan[Plan Usage]. In most cases, increasing your credit package minimizes repeat refills. To manage your plan, select *Plan Overview*.

[#scale-plan-auto-refill]
=== Scale plan

On the Scale plan, you can agree to use Credit Auto-Refill (CAR). When your usage reaches 20% of your credit purchase, you receive a notice that your credit is getting low. If your credit reaches 10% and you have not made another purchase, you receive a refill of either 25% (default) or 10% of your credit purchase.

For example:

. You buy 100M credits.
. You get a notice at 20M credits.
. CAR runs at 10M credits.

A supplemental purchase interrupts CAR.

If you decline CAR, you do not get a notice at 20%. If your credits deplete to 0, your service is interrupted until you make another purchase.

[#fixed-date-credit-allocation]
=== Fixed-date credit allocation

Your order may include credits that are scheduled to be added to your account at set intervals, for example, on a semi-annual basis. In this case, your credit purchase is divided equally between credit allocations and provisioned at the pre-assigned, regular intervals described on your order.

When your credit balance runs low and you have CAR enabled, CAR provides additional credits to keep your builds running until the next scheduled allocation arrives. CAR is billed separately from your order's credit allocation schedule.

If CAR is not enabled, your builds are blocked when your credits are exhausted, and remain blocked until the next scheduled credit allocation interval date.

For example:

. Your order is for 2 years and includes 400M credits split across 4 semi-annual allocations of 100M credits each.
. After nine months, you have used your available credits and your balance runs low before the next scheduled credit allocation interval date.
.. If CAR is *enabled*, CAR provides credits until your next 100M credit allocation is provisioned on its scheduled date.
.. If CAR is *not enabled*, builds are blocked until the next scheduled credit allocation interval date.

Contact your account team if you have questions about how your order is configured.

To view your credit allocation interval schedule and settings, select menu:Plan[Plan Overview] in the CircleCI web app.

[#free-plan-out-of-credits]
=== Free plan

On the Free plan, jobs fail to run when you run out of credits. See <<free-plan-limits,Free plan limits>>.

[#billing]
== Billing

You can pay for monthly pricing from inside the CircleCI web app.

On the Performance plan, at the beginning of your billing cycle, you are charged for premium support tiers and your monthly credit allocation. Credit refills during the month, such as auto-refilling at approximately 25% when there are 0 credits remaining, are paid at the time of the refill.

CircleCI charges your credit card on these dates:

* The day you upgrade to a paid plan or change paid plans.
* The monthly renewal date, if your team is on the monthly plan.
* The annual renewal date, if your team is on the annual plan.
* The last day of the month, if your team is on the annual plan and there is an outstanding balance from adding new users or using more credits.
* Any time your team's credit balance drops below your preset limit on the Performance plan, when another credit purchase is processed.

[#prepaid-billing]
=== Prepaid billing

Prepaid billing is a payment option for customers on the Performance plan. Prepaid billing allows you to purchase credits in bulk, pay upfront, and get discounted prices for credits based on volume purchased. Discounting starts at a purchase volume of 5 million credits.

When you use prepaid billing:

* Your credit card is charged immediately when you select prepaid billing. When you run out of credits, your credit package is automatically renewed at the purchase level you selected, and your credit card is charged again for the refill.
* You still receive 30,000 credits and 5 free user seats every month.
* If you switch from a monthly subscription, you keep access to any balance credits previously purchased, and the 12-month expiry date remains.

To switch to prepaid billing:

. Select *Plan* in the CircleCI web app.
. Select the *Manage* tab.
. Select *Set up and switch*.

To change your upcoming prepaid billing refill:

. Select *Plan* in the CircleCI web app.
. Select the *Manage* tab.
. Select the pencil icon in the *Credits* section to edit your auto refill rate.

[#troubleshooting-job-is-queued]
== Troubleshooting: Do I get charged if my job is "queued" or "preparing"?

No. If you are notified that a job is "queued", it indicates that your job is waiting due to a **plan** or **concurrency** limit. If your job indicates that it is "preparing", it means that CircleCI is setting up or _dispatching_ your job so that it may run.

If your jobs use the Docker executor and you find that they are "preparing" for quite some time, try using a more recent Docker image to reduce the delay. See the xref:execute:building-docker-images.adoc[Building Docker Images] page for more information.
